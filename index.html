05

Method

A fixed, reproducible
evaluation protocol.

  1. 01
    Sample

    Shuffle the Oxford-IIIT Pet test split with seed 42 and request 500 valid masks.

  2. 02
    Prompt

    Place one positive point at the deepest interior foreground pixel.

  3. 03
    Infer

    Upscale the short side to 1,280 px, run STT-B, and map the prediction back.

  4. 04
    Measure

    Score regions, boundaries, latency, GPU memory, geometry, confidence, and failures.
